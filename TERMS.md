Russian (`frontend/src/legal/`). If you change one, change the other. Read these terms together with

By creating an account or using LekseRadar you agree to these terms.

## What LekseRadar is

LekseRadar collects your homework from your school platforms in one place and helps you learn with an AI tutor,
quizzes and flashcards. It is free.

LekseRadar was started by a student team at the Oslo Sprint hackathon (6–8 November 2026) and is still being
developed. Features can change, and the service can be paused or shut down.

## Your account

- Use your own email address and keep your password to yourself.
- One account per person. Do not use someone else's account.
- You are responsible for what happens in your account. If you think someone else has used it, change your password.
- If you are under 13, you need a parent or guardian to agree before you use LekseRadar.

## Connecting school platforms

- Connect only your own accounts, and follow your school's rules and the platform's rules.
- The connections to itslearning and Visma InSchool are unofficial. They can stop working at any time.
- You can disconnect a platform at any time. This deletes its access, its assignments and the chats, quizzes and flashcards you made for them.
- Assignments belong to your school and teachers. LekseRadar only shows you a copy.

## The AI tutor, quizzes and flashcards

- Answers, quizzes and flashcards are made by an AI. They can be wrong or incomplete. Check important things with
  your teacher or your textbook.
- The AI tutor is meant to help you understand, not to do your homework for you. Follow your school's rules about
  getting help.
- Quiz results are for practice. They are not grades.
- Do not write sensitive personal information in the chat, such as your address, phone number or health. Chat text
  is sent to the AI service (see [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)).

## Not allowed

- Breaking the law, bullying or harassing anyone, or sharing other people's personal data.
- Trying to get into other accounts or to break or get around LekseRadar's security or limits.
- Overloading the service, for example with automated requests.
- Trying to make the AI produce harmful content.

We may close or limit an account that breaks these rules.

## Your content

What you write (chats and answers) is yours. We store and use it only to run LekseRadar for you, as described in
[PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

LekseRadar is provided "as is". We do our best, but we cannot promise that it works without errors or
interruptions, that reminders always arrive, or that AI answers are correct. As far as the law allows, the team is
not responsible for missed deadlines, lost data or decisions you make based on AI answers.

## Stopping

You can stop using LekseRadar at any time. Deleting your account on the site is not possible yet; until it is, ask
us to delete it (see Contact).
